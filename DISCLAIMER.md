# Disclaimer

The workbench is a tool for **exploring designs faster**: talking a design
through, looking at it, counting materials. It does not replace design
knowledge, engineering or a builder's judgement.

- **The output can be wrong.** Models, checks, shopping lists, cut lists,
  fixing counts and prices are made by scripts written with an AI, from
  rules of thumb and sources that may be incomplete or misread. The AI in
  the conversation can misunderstand you or make mistakes too.
- **Checks are guidance, not approval.** "Looks good" means the
  workbench's own checks passed. It does not mean a
  structure is safe or a part will hold. The timber checks are simple
  formulas, not structural engineering, and they don't know your site,
  your loads, your timber or your local rules.
- **You are responsible for what you make.** Check every design yourself
  before you print, order or build it. For anything people stand under,
  anything that carries snow or wind, anything that needs a permit, or
  anything that holds pressure, weight or heat, have a qualified person
  review it, and follow your local building rules.
- **Know what you are doing,** or work with someone who does. The
  workbench assumes you can judge whether a design makes sense.
- **Prices are a dated snapshot** of a supplier's public list, not an
  offer.
