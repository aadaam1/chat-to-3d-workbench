================================================================
  GARDEN TAP AERATOR - PRINTING INSTRUCTIONS
  Everything you need is in this folder. Nothing to design.
================================================================

WHAT IT IS
  A small plastic nozzle that screws onto a garden tap and makes
  the water come out as an even stream instead of a spiral.

  It is ONE part. No assembly, no supports, no glue.


----------------------------------------------------------------
  STEP 1 - PRINT THE THREE GAUGES FIRST
----------------------------------------------------------------

  Folder:  1-gauges-print-these-first/
  Files:   gauge_0.25.stl, gauge_0.30.stl, gauge_0.35.stl
  Time:    about 30 minutes for all three, ~18 g

  These are little test rings. They check that the printed
  thread fits the real tap. Print all three, hand them over,
  and we will tell you which number fits.

  If you would rather do it in one go, print gauge_0.30.stl
  plus the part below - 0.30 is right on most machines.


----------------------------------------------------------------
  STEP 2 - PRINT THE PART
----------------------------------------------------------------

  Folder:  2-the-part/
  Qty:     one of EACH of the two files below
  Time:    about 1 h each, ~30-40 g

    aerator_G1_fit0.30.stl            standard, 51 mm tall
    aerator_G1_fit0.30_COMPACT.stl    9 mm shorter, 42 mm tall
    (.step of each: the same parts as CAD solids, for Fusion)

  Changed after the last print: the thread is 5 mm instead of 11,
  to match the tap's short thread, so the top no longer stands
  proud and holds water. The compact one also has a shorter
  chamber; print both and keep whichever gives the better stream.


----------------------------------------------------------------
  SETTINGS - Bambu Studio
----------------------------------------------------------------

  MATERIAL       ASA.  It lives outdoors in the sun.
                 PETG is an acceptable substitute.
                 NOT PLA - it creeps under water pressure and
                 falls apart in UV within a season.

  ORIENTATION    As supplied. Drop it on the plate and do not
                 rotate it. The flat face with all the small
                 holes goes DOWN on the bed, threaded end up.
                 The inside is laid out for exactly this - turn
                 it and it needs support in a cavity you can
                 never get the support out of.

  SUPPORTS       None. Turn them OFF.

  LAYER HEIGHT   0.16 mm     (0.20 works, 0.16 seals better)

  WALL LOOPS     5           <- the important one. This part
                                holds mains water pressure and
                                must not weep through its walls.

  TOP/BOTTOM     6 layers
  INFILL         30% gyroid
  SPEED          slow the outer walls to 40-50 mm/s

  Everything else: stock profile is fine.


----------------------------------------------------------------
  HOW TO TELL IT PRINTED PROPERLY
----------------------------------------------------------------

  - The bottom face has 36 holes, all open. Hold it up to a
    light. A 2.0 mm drill bit should pass through them.
  - Look in the threaded end: a post up the middle and four blades
    in a cross, and straight down to the holes. No plate.
  - The thread should look clean, not stringy.
  - Nothing should rattle. It is one solid piece.
